You are evaluating one potential next response in a dialogue.

Your task is to rate the response on Engagingness using a 1 to 3 scale.

Evaluation Criteria:
Engagingness (1-3): Measures how effectively the response sustains interest and encourages further dialogue.
- 1 (Dull/Conversation-Stopping): The response is overly generic, evasive, repetitive, or fundamentally shuts down the conversation. It forces the other person to carry the entire burden or leaves no clear path forward. Reserve this score only for responses that clearly halt progress or are fundamentally unengaging.
- 2 (Acceptable/Mildly Engaging): The response is polite, relevant, and maintains conversational flow. It acknowledges the topic naturally and leaves room for the partner to continue. A score of 2 is appropriate for most natural conversational turns that do not actively kill the conversation. Brevity alone does not indicate a lack of engagement if the reply feels natural and keeps the interaction moving.
- 3 (Highly Engaging): The response actively sustains interest by introducing specific details, asking a thoughtful follow-up, sharing a relevant insight, or presenting an interesting perspective. It naturally invites a reply and demonstrates clear conversational momentum beyond simple acknowledgment.

Evaluation Steps:
1. Analyze the conversation history to grasp the topic, tone, and flow.
2. Assess whether the response keeps the conversation alive. Do not downgrade a response simply for being concise or lacking extensive detail. Focus on whether it functions as a natural conversational turn that maintains momentum.
3. Distinguish between a basic conversational turn (Score 2) and one that actively drives the dialogue forward (Score 3). Reserve Score 1 only for responses that clearly halt progress or are fundamentally unengaging.
4. Assign exactly one score from 1, 2, or 3 based on how effectively the response sustains interest.
5. Format your output strictly: provide a brief rationale explaining your rating, followed immediately by a final line containing only the score in this exact format:
Score: <1, 2, or 3>

Do not output scores outside this scale. Ensure the rationale and score line are the only content in your response.
